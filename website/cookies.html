Cookie policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

This website does not set cookies and does not use similar technologies (local storage, tracking pixels or fingerprinting) to track you. That's why you won't see a cookie banner here.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site, may set a strictly necessary security cookie (such as __cf_bm) to tell people from bots and protect the site from abuse. These cookies don't track you across sites and are exempt from consent requirements. See Cloudflare's cookie list.
  • GitHub sets cookies only when you visit github.com itself, for example to download the installer or read the code. See GitHub's cookie policy.

The app

The SwarmCode desktop app stores its settings and encrypted sign-in tokens on your computer. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Questions? See the privacy policy or open an issue.
